A Weekend in Verona

The city of Romeo and Juliet, wandered on foot. From the crenellated Portoni della Brà and the brick Torre dei Lamberti over Piazza dei Signori, down to the Adige rushing beneath Castel San Pietro and the arches of the Ponte Pietra. Terracotta rooftops, statue-lined loggias, and the courtyard of Casa di Giulietta with its bronze Juliet and the balcony that made her famous.
